QR Attendance

About

This app allows teachers to quickly take attendance by generating a random QR code. Students scan the QR code, are redirected to a short form where they enter name, grade, and section, and the teacher can view a live list of who scanned and submitted their info.

Get Started

Teachers: create an account or sign in to create QR codes and see attendance.

Teacher sign in / dashboard

PWA

This site is a Progressive Web App. Install it on your device for offline access to the teacher dashboard (cached pages).
